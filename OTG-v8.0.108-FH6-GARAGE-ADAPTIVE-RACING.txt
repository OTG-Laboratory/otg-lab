OTG! v8.0.108 — FH6 GARAGE-ADAPTIVE RACING (QA BUILD)
BASELINE: v8.0.107 ACCEPTED. This is the FH6 prelaunch final prep pass.

WHAT IS NEW
- FH6 Festival and Race Off pages present a current-Garage-only competition picker.
- COMPETITION OPPORTUNITIES AUTO-UPDATE when FH6 catalogue cars are ticked/unticked.
- Manufacturer, Era, Class, Favourite, all-NORMAL, and specialist competitions (FE Road, FE Off-Road, Drift Zones, dedicated Race, Off-Road / Buggy).
- Eligibility uses Specialist-First classification: every known normal group excludes specialist cars. Unresolved cars cannot be included and FE needs a confirmed ROAD/OFF-ROAD role.
- Classifications that remain draft are clearly labelled, not silently treated as verified.
- A competition needs at least two eligible owned cars; no arbitrary Garage ownership milestones.
- A random starting-order REHEARSAL; redraws do not change Garage or past competitions.
- Workload previews respect 24 results per Festival visit / 5 pairings per Race Off visit. For 25+ entrants, group-stage preview is not presented as a completed championship.
- FH6 Garage gets OPEN GARAGE RACING shortcut, and its Festival/Race Off navigation leads to this adaptive picker.
- Evil List 2 — Return of the Twat! is PARKED, not generated or started. The 715-visit earlier Evil blueprint remains a separate planning view and does not control everyday racing.

CRITICAL PRELAUNCH RESTRICTIONS
- START COMPETITION is intentionally DISABLED pending real FH6 routes, Drift Zones, LOCATION verification and the playable result engine. This update prepares eligibility and UI; it does NOT claim playable races already work.
- Once actual START is enabled in a future FH6 build, it must freeze entrant IDs, car order and verified tracks. The freeze helper is implemented & tested but is not connected to a playable Start button yet.
- Route pools remain empty by default; FH5 tracks are NEVER reused for FH6.
- No automatic creation of runs, tournament results, Evil List 2 slots, or Records entries.
- No resets; no change to existing FH5 and GT7 data or current Space.
