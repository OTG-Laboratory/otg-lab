Three NEW tiles at top of GARAGE:
- FORGOTTEN CARS: lists owned cars with no saved timed results, then those with old timestamped results; configurable 30/60/90/180 days. "Never recorded" does not mean never driven in the actual game.
- SMART CAR FINDER: search, make, class/type, decade, racing activity filters, and random draw from matching owned cars. Never draws unowned catalogue entries; never sets up or runs a race.
- CAR COMPARISON: pick two owned cars; show saved timed result count, distinct recorded routes, latest race and per-car PBs on ONLY identical named tracks/layouts. No comparing unrelated routes, PI/performance estimates or made-up car ratings.

Both the read-only analyser and UI query currently active Space only (FH5, GT7, FH6 or manual Spaces). Spaces with zero recorded results get truthful empty states. Drift Zone score data excluded because this is a timed-result feature.
