BASELINE

RACE OFF VARIANT LABELS
- Frozen Race Off snapshots now resolve back to their FH5 catalogue identity for display.
- Forza Edition cars are explicitly labelled (for example: Morris Minor 1000 1953 • FORZA EDITION).
- Repair is presentation-first and applies to existing saved brackets as well as new Race Offs.
- Applies across bracket draw/progress, ENTER RESULT, next-racer transition, tournament history and champion views.
- Underlying immutable car IDs and race results are not changed.

CAR HISTORY — CHAMPIONSHIP CAREER
- Adds a Championship Career section above Honours and PBs.
- Summary: Championships Entered, Complete, Wins, Podiums and Best Championship Finish.
- Standard championships show exact finishing position / field size, rounds, cumulative time and best single-round placing.
- Evil Campaign championships show their Slot number.
- Active/abandoned championships remain visible with status.
- Groups and Swiss runs show format-appropriate final/elimination context rather than inventing a false overall placing.
- Career rows are derived live from saved results, so Results / Corrections automatically update the displayed championship finish.

NO RACING-DATA DUPLICATION
- No separate championship-history database is introduced. Car History is calculated from the existing run/results data.
