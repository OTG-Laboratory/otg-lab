RaceHub v5.7.51 — CHAMPIONSHIP DISCOVERY

Adds one-time, non-blocking Championship discovery guidance:
- Triggered only when a Garage change makes a Manufacturer, Era, or Favourite Manufacturer Championship newly eligible (minimum 2 cars).
- Message: NEW CHAMPIONSHIP AVAILABLE, Championship name, eligibility explanation.
- Actions: NOT NOW and VIEW IN FESTIVAL.
- Discovery is remembered per RaceHub Space and will not be announced again after eligibility later drops and returns.
- Existing Festival eligibility behaviour remains unchanged.
- Existing v5.7.50 race-night repairs and user data format are preserved.
